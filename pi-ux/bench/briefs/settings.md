Design the settings screen for "Relay", a team notification-routing web app (desktop browser). Sections: profile (name, email, avatar upload), notification channels (email / Slack / webhook with enable toggles and per-channel test buttons), quiet hours (time-range picker with timezone note, day-of-week selector), and a danger zone (delete account with confirmation input). Every control needs its real interaction states: default, hover, focus-visible, disabled, and one control mid-error (invalid webhook URL with an inline error message).
